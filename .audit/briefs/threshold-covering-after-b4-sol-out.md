## Design red-flags screen

The alternate-source adapter leaks storage and clock representations through a new interface and fails source admission before design depth matters. The scenario family adds a public choice that no exact verdict can use. The roll guard leaks future session state. A four-cell branch is information leakage and a point patch. A configurable completion framework is a shallow module because the caller still has to decide which invented cash law to trust.

Lazy resolution is the only clean interface. It keeps commitment separate from terminal truth and asks for an outcome only after admission. Its probe still fired because every missing outcome is needed. A good interface cannot manufacture absent evidence.

## Principles that changed a decision

- `principle-exhaust-the-design-space` and `principle-codebase-design`. The arena compared whole shapes rather than variants of skipping four cells. The comparison exposed lazy outcome resolution as the clean seam, then the admission probe killed it on evidence.
- `principle-redesign-from-first-principles`. Commitment and terminal truth were treated as separate facts. This ruled out READY filtering and cell-specific repair.
- `principle-fix-root-causes`. The cause is a partial terminal-outcome law on admitted entries. The map does not patch the four symptoms.
- `principle-laziness-protocol` and `principle-subtract-before-you-add`. No scorer, engine adapter, completion framework, or regenerated store is added when none can satisfy the charter.
- `principle-prove-it-works`, `principle-sequence-verifiable-units`, and the fast-enough rule. Four small checks ran before any full walk. Three fired the relevant STOP, and the one green check proved representation only.
- `principle-never-block-on-the-human`. The reversible covering decision is made here. No clarification is requested and no unauthorized charter change is assumed.
- `principle-separate-before-serializing-shared-state`. Arena candidates were read-only and owned no shared output. This page is the single synthesis write.
