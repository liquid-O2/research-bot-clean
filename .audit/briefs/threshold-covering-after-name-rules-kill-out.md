1. `tape_flow_with`: argmax of side times window signed-flow sum.
2. `tape_flow_against`: argmin of the same. Direction control.
3. `tape_imbalance_with`: argmax of side times mean over valid seconds of (bid_sz - ask_sz) / (bid_sz + ask_sz).
4. `tape_imbalance_against`: argmin. Control.
5. `tape_drift_with`: argmax of side times (g0_mid at last valid window second minus g0_mid at first valid window second).
6. `tape_drift_against`: argmin. G1 candidates are zigzag reversals, so against-drift is the theory-loaded direction; its pair is the control.
