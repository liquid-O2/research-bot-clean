# Why live entry misses the cell-best name

Synthesized 2026-08-26 from `.audit/briefs/threshold-why-source-out.md` (source control) and `.audit/briefs/threshold-why-docs-out.md` (long-form docs). Every load-bearing citation below was re-opened and spot-checked against the receipt, commit, or page it names. Bounds: entry only, one mini contract, no exits, no extra size. Teacher-cash cannot promote. 2021 cannot promote.

### The Question

Why does live one-contract entry take a name that is not the cell-best, and why do the already-tried instruments (the skill-free CLEAR orders, the E1R heads, the roster fields, the T53/T54 forecasts) fail to recover that identity?

### The Code in Question

- `.audit/score_threshold_2022_2024_read.py` lines 222-237, `pick_cell_names`. The live pick. Keeps the row with the smallest `(decision_ts_ns, candidate_id)` per (asset, d8, phase) cell among CLEAR-eligible candidates.
- `.audit/score_threshold_2022_2024_ceiling.py` lines 163-172, `pick_cell_best_ready`. The hindsight twin. Keeps the READY teacher row with the largest `cert_close_usd` per cell.
- `.audit/threshold-2022-2024-freeze.md`. The page that binds the live rule. All three anchors first appear in commit `2a7e301` (Russell Cleanroom Migration, 2026-08-26 14:09:51 +0000, a 58-file dump).

### What We Found

Why the live name is not cell-best:

- **[Direct]** Live entry takes the earliest CLEAR candidate because the freeze binds it to that. "Enter one contract in every joinable cell (asset, d8, phase) on every selected day, taking each cell's earliest CLEAR candidate", pick "the eligible candidate with the smallest `decision_ts_ns`; tie-break the lexicographically smallest `candidate_id`". Source: `.audit/threshold-2022-2024-freeze.md` lines 7 and 14 (commit `2a7e301`); verbatim in `.audit/threshold-2022-2024-read.json` `frozen_rule`.
- **[Direct]** The freeze's written reason is that no live instrument can supply the name. The one live forecast "is day-level and assetless, so it cannot pick the name and cannot rank assets or phases" (freeze line 13), the pick "is arrival order behind a compliance filter, not a fitted field rule" (line 48), and the forbidden-formulas list bans every fitted alternative (Ticket 28 hold, Ticket 39 location-ranker, E1R ENTER-weight, roster fields, enter-all; line 48). The pre-bound kill sentence names the gap outright. "forecast day-gate plus a skill-free name pick did not clear the rungs; the unmeasured lever is within-cell name selection, which has no instrument (T53/T54)" (freeze line 32; `.audit/threshold-2022-2024-read.json` `dollar_stop.kill_sentence`, verdict KILL).
- **[Direct]** The forecast instruments say the same about themselves. "It does not pick the name. Ticket 53 established that predicting cell value does not locate the picker's failures, and nothing here changes that." Source: `design/entry_reset/T54_FORWARD_VOL_20260823.md` lines 94-97.
- **[Direct]** The miss is measured, and it is identity, not timing polarity or cost. Earliest CLEAR matches cell-best in 149 of 1732 cells (match_rate 0.0860); the winner sits at mean time rank 28.22 in a mean cell of 105.49 CLEAR names; latest and cheapest also miss. Earliest reads HG -99.10, NKD -68.80, SI -162.51 usd per asset-day (1734 trades) while the same-join cell-best line reads HG 2758.95, NKD 3815.22, SI 3880.47 and clears the rungs. The receipt's own sentence: "The miss is within-cell identity, not a missing ceiling, and not first-versus-last or frozen_cost." Sources: `.audit/threshold-capture-gap.json` `capture`, `lines.*`, `dollar_stop.applied`; commit `2a7e301` body "Earliest CLEAR matches the winner in 8.6% of cells. Latest and cheapest miss too."
- **[Direct]** Cell-best is a hindsight object by construction and is labelled non-promotable. The ceiling module docstring reads "Exploratory hindsight cell-best ceiling. Throwaway audit. Cannot promote."; `pick_cell_best_ready` needs the READY teacher row's `cert_close_usd`, which the live pick is forbidden to see before selection (teacher join only on selected names, freeze line 16). Sources: `.audit/score_threshold_2022_2024_ceiling.py` lines 1-51 and 163-172; `.audit/threshold-2022-2024-ceiling.json` `dollar_stop.verdict` PROCEED, `gated.clears_rungs` true (the source slice wrote `gated_clears_rungs`; the actual key is `gated.clears_rungs`).

Why each tried instrument fails to recover the identity:

- **[Direct]** The frozen E1R head never emits a name at all. `named_cause` = `action_regret_head_never_prefers_enter`, `enter_preference.selected_opportunity_total` = 0, `fit_capture.training_capture_pass` = false (0.0 to 0.43 percent against a 0.9 target), every advantage grid negative. "The connected walk never prefers ENTER while the same-window teacher ceiling already clears HG 2000, NKD 1500, and SI 1500." A head that never enters publishes no identity to be right or wrong about. Sources: `.audit/threshold-enter-gap-20260825.json`; commits `46a3cd9`, `6e0a535`, `561f6cb` ("the bottleneck is not a matrix-versus-walk feature mismatch").
- **[Direct]** E1R's own mechanism was diagnosed as not an identity instrument. Regret is "a substitution margin of order $11 to $38 measured against an oracle continuation ... the furthest of all of them from the trade", "argmin over a flat label is noise, and that is the shape of the E1R $0", and the learner never grouped by cell. The 2026-08-22 diagnosis closed it: the E1R defect "explains $0 vs ~$500, not $500 vs $2,000", "Do not relitigate it as the live problem." Sources: `design/entry_reset/OPUS5_MAX_LABEL_DIAGNOSIS.md` lines 70-79, 104-115, 241-244; `design/entry_reset/DIAGNOSIS_20260822.md` lines 35-37; `design/entry_reset/overview.md` lines 36-37, 111-112.
- **[Direct]** When refit E1R-family heads do enter, they take the wrong names. H5 official E1R lands cell-best 0/31 and top-2 1/31 (`majority_top2` false); H3 cell-best 21/142, top-2 44/142; H7 cell-best 16/139, top-2 30/139. Dollars fail every rung (H3 $227.50 on 142 trades; H5 $426.25 on 31; H7 -$2051.25 on 139). Sources: `.audit/threshold-h5-top2.json` `h5_conclusion`, `variants.*.overall`; `.audit/threshold-hillclimb.tsv` rows 7-17; `.audit/threshold-refit-h5-official-e1r.json` `slices.all_rows.pred_enter_count` 755.
- **[Direct]** The roster single-field veto is a recorded KILL. 216 rules scanned, 0 survived a bar stated before the scan (remove more than half of walked event-not-top-2 ENTERs while keeping more than half of walked top-2). The best rule, `event_order > 0`, keeps 73.3% (55/75) but removes only 27.7% (38/137). Separation AUCs run 0.455 to 0.497, near chance. Sources: `.audit/threshold-roster-kill.json` `kill_bar`, `chosen_rule`, `separation`; `.audit/briefs/roster-kill.md` line 48; first appears in `2a7e301`.
- **[Direct]** Roster-style restriction had already failed as an identity instrument on the 2021 confirmation line. "Candidate acceptance, candidate rank, candidate value, lawful value rank, and top-k roster restriction have all been tried. They do not identify which extreme event caused the cell value." Source: `design/entry_reset/55-entry-v2-recovery-plan/confirmation-receipt-audit.md` lines 53-81, 156-184.
- **[Direct]** Even an oracle cannot rescue an arrival-order pick. On 2021 TRAIN, knowing the finished cell's winning side and taking the earliest name on it still misses the rung ("Those dollars are oracles ... Even that misses the rung"); the cell-max is first-born in 21% / 6% / 12% of cells (HG/NKD/SI) and becomes eligible a median 41/42/37 minutes after the first keep-first name; "Side-then-earliest is an oracle and still misses the rung." Sources: `design/entry_reset/HANDOFF_DECISION_PLANE_20260822.md` lines 21-43; `design/entry_reset/SELECTION_HOLD_EXTREME_20260822.md` lines 10-22.

Contradictions in the record, both sides cited:

- **[Direct]** Two cause strings exist for the enter gap. The frozen receipt says `action_regret_head_never_prefers_enter` (`6e0a535`); the generator that would reprint it now returns `e1r_regret_head_never_prefers_enter_on_any_walked_window` (`1ae7933`; `.audit/assert_threshold_replay_receipt.py` lines 260-270). The published json was never rewritten.
- **[Direct]** "Never prefers ENTER" describes only the frozen head. The refit H5 official E1R head predicts ENTER on 755 rows and walks 31 trades (`.audit/threshold-refit-h5-official-e1r.json`; `.audit/threshold-hillclimb.tsv` rows 10-11). Both wear the name E1R in the trail. They are different runs.
- **[Direct]** The design tree closed E1R ("Do not relitigate it as the live problem", `DIAGNOSIS_20260822.md` line 37) while `.audit/threshold-path-to-rungs.md` lines 1-4 still treat the frozen E1R head as the established THRESHOLD bottleneck on 2026-08-26. No page reconciles the reopening.
- **[Direct]** The 2021 record holds both "winners form early" (tercile winner share about 0.30 / 0.20 / 0.07, "last-formed is never best", `DIAGNOSIS_20260822.md` lines 47-50) and "the paying name is usually a later zigzag" (~40 minutes after the first keep-first name, `SELECTION_HOLD_EXTREME_20260822.md` lines 10-15). These are different name universes (formation rank among all names versus eligibility order among keep-first names), and the 2022-2024 CLEAR-row time rank 28 of 105 is a third universe. The docs never join them.
- **[Direct]** The freeze says "Frozen 2026-08-26 before any 2022+ outcome dollar was parsed" (line 3), yet it landed in the same commit `2a7e301` as the receipts that parse those dollars (read, capture-gap, ceiling). Git holds no ordering inside one commit. The same commit body says "Stop re-proving a ceiling we already have" while adding the ceiling script and its PROCEED receipt.

### What We Can Reasonably Infer

- **[Inferred]** Why "earliest" specifically, rather than latest, cheapest, or random. No contemporaneous rationale exists anywhere in the record. Given that the freeze needed a causal, deterministic, unfitted pick (line 48), and that the 2021 design line had repeatedly reached for earliest as its default (the within-side rule "enter the earliest keep-first name on the called side" justified by "the best-value series forms early, median formation-rank fraction .16 to .25", `FABLE5_XHIGH_LABEL_DIAGNOSIS.md` lines 117-123; the DIAGNOSIS anatomy "winners form early ... last-formed is never best"), earliest appears to be an inherited 2021 default rather than a measured 2022-2024 choice. The measurement that latest and cheapest also miss came only afterward, in the capture-gap receipt.
- **[Inferred]** Why no tried instrument recovers the identity. Each one reads a different axis than within-cell name rank. T53/T54 rank days and cells (allocation); the frozen E1R head ranks actions on isolated rows and never grouped by cell; roster fields are arrival-order counters whose top-2 separation is near chance; earliest, latest, and cheapest are time and cost orders, and the receipt says the miss "is not first-versus-last or frozen_cost". The evidence is consistent with the kill sentence's claim that within-cell name selection has never had an instrument pointed at it on the 2022-2024 join. The one 2021 attempt aimed at identity, the T39 location-ranker, reached about half the rung and reduces to entry-price order within a side (ticket 44 banner in `T39_VERDICT_20260823.md`).
- **[Inferred]** Why arrival order in particular fails. The winner is late in every universe measured. Mean time rank 28 of 105 CLEAR names on 2022-2024, and eligibility a median ~40 minutes after the first keep-first name on 2021 events, suggest the paying identity resolves well after the cell's first arrivals, so any first-arrival rule likely samples the cell before its winner exists. The two measurements come from different name universes, so this is a convergent reading, not one number.

### Competing Hypotheses

Why the freeze bound a pick it had reason to think would miss:

- **Hypothesis A.** Earliest CLEAR was a deliberate skill-free floor to make the day-gate falsifiable, with identity knowingly sacrificed. Evidence for: the pre-bound kill sentence already names within-cell selection as the unmeasured lever (freeze line 32); the forbidden-formulas paragraph bans every fitted pick (line 48); the commissioning brief ordered "Forecast cannot pick the name. Say so" (`.audit/briefs/threshold-2022-2024-freeze.md` lines 3, 17). Evidence against or missing: the freeze never says "we expect earliest to miss"; no sentence weighs earliest against another skill-free order.
- **Hypothesis B.** Earliest was believed a decent proxy because the 2021 anatomy said winners form early. Evidence for: tercile winner share 0.30 / 0.20 / 0.07 and "last-formed is never best" (`DIAGNOSIS_20260822.md`); formation-rank fraction .16 to .25 (`FABLE5_XHIGH_LABEL_DIAGNOSIS.md`). Evidence against: the same era's pages say the payer is a later zigzag and that side-then-earliest misses even as an oracle; the freeze cites none of this either way.
- Both can be true at once (a floor chosen where the prior looked least bad). The record does not force a winner.

Whether the freeze predates the dollar parse:

- **Hypothesis C.** The freeze text was written before any 2022+ outcome dollar was parsed, as its own line 3 claims, and the receipts were published later in one push.
- **Hypothesis D.** The dump commit mixed a pre-dollar draft with post-dollar numbers. Evidence for: freeze, kill receipt, capture-gap, and ceiling all first appear in `2a7e301` at 14:09:51 +0000. Evidence against: nothing contradicts the freeze's claim; it is simply unverifiable from git.

### Confidence Summary

The core answer is Direct and double-sourced. Live entry misses the cell-best name because the freeze bound a deliberately skill-free arrival-order pick after recording that within-cell name selection "has no instrument (T53/T54)", and the capture-gap receipt then measured that order finding the winner in 149 of 1732 cells (8.6%, winner mean time rank 28 of 105 CLEAR names). Each tried instrument's failure is also Direct: the frozen E1R head emits zero names, the refit heads that do enter land cell-best 0/31 to 21/142, roster fields separate top-2 at AUC 0.455 to 0.497, and even oracle side-then-earliest misses the rung on 2021. Why "earliest" was the chosen skill-free order is only Inferred (an inherited 2021 default; no contemporaneous rationale exists), and whether the freeze truly predates the dollar parse is Unknown, since freeze and receipts share one commit. No PR, issue, chat, observability, error-tracking, or warehouse channel was available to tighten either gap.
