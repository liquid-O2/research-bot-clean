# Fog and frontier

Use this on work too big for one session, when the way to the destination is not visible yet.

**Name the destination first.** One or two lines. It fixes scope.

The plan is an **index**, not a store. Each decision lives in one place. The overview gists and links. It does not restate.

**Fog of war** is in-scope work you cannot specify yet. Write it under Not yet specified. Do not pre-slice fog into fake tickets. Ticket a question only when you can state it precisely now, even if you cannot answer it yet.

**Out of scope** is past the destination. It never graduates. Close mis-scoped tickets rather than walking them.

The **frontier** is the unblocked, unclaimed work you can take now. Do not charge at the destination through the fog.

One decision per session unless the tickets are independent research that can run in parallel. Resolving a ticket graduates whatever fog it made specifiable.

If grilling a destination surfaces no fog, you do not need a map. Stay in the narrower playbook.
