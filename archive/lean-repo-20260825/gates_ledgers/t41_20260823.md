# Gates: ticket 41 — make R6 faster, losslessly, and adopt it

Scope: the user asked for any lossless efficiency gain on R6, then adoption.
R6 is measured at 1.85x (3.8616 ms/row vs the oracle's 7.1473). Every gain here
must be proven byte-identical by the standing differential, not argued.

Law: bit-identity is the acceptance (D-017). `-ffp-contract=off` stays. No
`-ffast-math`, no `-fassociative-math`, nothing that may reorder a float64
reduction. A flag that changes one byte is reverted, not explained.
