## W21-PIN-2 (2026-08-11, orchestrator ruling on the Greek-residual denominator + width normalization)
The denominator for "σρ-oriented resid_bps" (Q6/Q7) is the UNDERLYING eligible-NBBO mid at the span start: resid_bps = (Δmid_obs − [δ·Δu + ½γ(Δu)²]) / u_mid × 1e4.
