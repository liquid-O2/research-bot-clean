## 0. Where we are, in five sentences

The goal is an entry policy for SI/HG/NKD futures that clears >$2,000 per asset-day (HG) and
>$1,500 (NKD, SI) on held pre-2025H2 blocks, at ≥80% of the exact delayed-candidate ceiling,
with at most 12 trades per portfolio-day. Candidates (reversals at extremes) are generated by
a frozen causal generator; the unsolved part is choosing which candidate to enter and when.
Today's measurements established that nothing decidable at candidate formation works, that a
confirmation signal accrues over the 300 s watch window but is far too weak with the current
features, and that every causal decision-time rule tried inside that window fails on held
blocks while a hindsight pick of the phase's most extended candidate would keep 37–60% of the
ceiling. The remaining levers are a longer window (600 s), the book's unbuilt order-flow
ingredients, time-remaining conditioning, and a phase-scale sequential decision object. The
user decides which build to fund; until then nothing launches.

## 4. Facts established today (tiered; receipts above)

Direct (measured, preregistered, controls beside them):
1. Nothing separates winners from losers at candidate formation (D2 chance; accrual Δ=0 ≈ .50
   on every asset).
2. A confirmation signal accrues over the watch window: the unit-weight, side-resolved,
   sign-oriented composite of the four-state ingredients reaches within-cell AUC ≈ .60 HG /
   .56 NKD / .62 SI at 290 s (winner ≥$600 vs loser ≤$0 series), reproduced when ingredients
   are selected on train days only; still rising at the 300 s cap.
3. Fitting does not help at this signal-to-noise: a regularized logistic fit ties the unit
   weights; tree models on the full 1,764-column plane do worse (D6 arms; the loop). The
   OWN/OPP side swap must be a feature transform, not something a model learns.
4. A winner-vs-loser classifier's .63–.65 AUC is `phase_remaining_sec`: series formed early
   in a phase have more time to reach $600 before the phase-close exit. Picking by it
   realizes nothing. Any value label must condition on time remaining.
5. Precision ruler (cell-oracle frame): a one-shot top-of-cell picker needs the per-candidate
   value error σ ≤ $302 HG / $150 NKD / $183 SI for 80% capture. This is a requirement on
   score quality — NOT a trade count, NOT dollars per day. Every picker here enters ≤1 per
   cell (≤9 per day), inside the 12-trade cap.
6. Hindsight location: picking the phase's most extended candidate beyond the prior-session
   range keeps .39/.51/.60 of the forward ceiling (≈$1,130/$1,050/$1,355 per asset-day) on
   every block — but the pick competes with candidates that form later (lookahead).
7. Five causal decision-time shapes inside the 300 s window fail on threshold and forward
   (at or below random; knobs from train only): extension threshold, running max,
   extension × confirmation composite, time-patience, re-test of a held extreme.
8. The D6/D7 cell-pick numbers share the cell-oracle frame: ranking diagnostics, not causal
   capture.

Supported (exploratory, not preregistered — steer design only):
9. The phase extreme is set mid-sequence (median formation rank .42–.66) and holds while
   17–26 later candidates form; the last-formed candidate is never the best; the oracle pick
   is a ≥$600 winner 27–34% of the time; big winners form early (winner share by formation
   tercile ≈ .30/.20/.07).
