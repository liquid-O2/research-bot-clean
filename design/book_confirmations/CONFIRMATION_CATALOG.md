## A · Confirmation predicates (computable from the existing stream)

Each: predicate sketch · timing band · source (digest, entry) · feature families.
Cross-lane agreement noted — blind lanes converging on one rule is the strongest
extraction signal we have.

- **CONF-REWARD-3T** — after a defense/absorption event at the extreme, signed
  displacement toward the trade ≥3 ticks within a short window; without it ~27% of
  absorptions fail (book's own stat, its ONLY numeric threshold; surfaced INDEPENDENTLY by
  3 lanes: mech-A #1, mech-B R3, sessions p24). Seconds. disc_evt_* + w15_*.
- **CONF-REPLENISH** — the defended level refreshes while still being hit; replenishment
  run ≥3; 1-2 = the named fake-out zone (mech-B R3; sessions REFRESH-CONSISTENCY; micro
  C3). Seconds-to-~2min over 2-3 cycles. disc_evt_reloads + disc_quote_* rebuild.
- **CONF-4STAGE** — ordered state machine: initial defense → replenishment (≥3) →
  attacker exhaustion (attack-rate decay + short-window delta turning against them) →
  lift-off (own-side aggression + immediate displacement). "Failed and confirmed
  absorption look identical at the moment most people enter; the difference only exists
  in time" (mech-B R3; mech-A #5 same machine). Few bars / minutes. Composable from
  disc_evt_*, disc_quote_*, w15/w60, disc_tclock.
- **CONF-EFFORT-NORESULT** — opposing aggression into the extreme with ~zero displacement
  (flow-vs-move divergence): unpaid aggression = trapped inventory = fuel. The mechanism
  every lane extracted; also the veto mirror (OUR side unpaid = warning). All timing
  bands. w{15..600}_* signed flow vs displacement.
- **CONF-OPP-AGGRESSION** — after absorption, the OTHER side's aggressive events actually
  arrive — "not just an absence of the first side" (mech-A #5). Seconds-minutes. Signed
  disc_evt_* flip.
- **CONF-OPP-EXHAUST** — the attacking side decelerates bar-over-bar into the extreme
  (speed-of-tape shrink; digit-thinning analog = attack/reload magnitude trend)
  (sessions EXHAUSTION-INTO-LEVEL + DOM digits; micro C2 absorption-vs-exhaustion
  discriminator). ~2-5 min. disc_tclock/vclock slope + disc_evt_* intensity trend.
- **CONF-TAPE-DEATH** — tape speed simply dies at the extension ("the absence of result
  is itself the signal — the quieter, more common version", mech-A #19). Seconds-minutes.
  disc_tclock/vclock collapse + near-zero disc_evt_*.
- **CONF-SECOND-DEFENSE** — the same extreme defended a second time with equal/greater
  conviction, price staying in the zone: "a level becomes the trade when the same side
  defends it a second time" (sessions OFM-second-defense; mech-B C5: control changed only
  after the second failed test). Minutes (observed ~10+ min). disc_level_z* repeat
  defense + disc_evt_reloads.
- **CONF-CANDLE-DELTA-DISAGREE** — displacement sign vs signed-flow sign disagree at the
  extreme (bearish flow, price holds/rises = bullish absorption). Proof lands per closed
  bar; reversal ~3-4 one-minute bars after first disagreement (micro C6). Minutes.
  w-window return × signed flow at disc_level_z*.
- **CONF-DELTA-DIVERGE / EXHAUST-PRINT** — new price extreme without a new flow extreme
  (builds over several swings, minutes) or a max-flow bar with zero forward displacement
  ("the turn is usually one or two candles away") (micro C8). Minutes. Flow envelope
  across w{60..1800}_* vs price-path extremes; disc_vclock burst.
- **CONF-CVD-SIDE** — cumulative session flow on the trade's side of its running median;
  slow variant: price holds while cumulative flow grinds against the level for tens of
  minutes = the level being consumed (sessions SESSION-CVD; mech-A #3). Minutes-session.
  Chained w1800_* (exact session-cumulative line is a small builder gap).
- **CONF-REFILL-VS-PRINTS** — rebuilds conditioned on traded volume at the level: refresh
  WITH prints = real defense (iceberg); refresh/vanish WITHOUT prints = theatre
  (spoof/layer) — "the single most computable novel discriminator" (micro beyond-book).
  Seconds. disc_quote_* rebuild × disc_evt_* volume ratio.
- **CONF-STACK-COUNT** — integer tell count over the predicates above: "one tell is
  interest, two is a trade" (micro C9). The book's own composite is a small-integer
  score, not a binary — natural model feature.
- **STATE-ARRIVAL-SPEED** — how the extreme was reached: fast/aggressive arrival →
  expect defense (fade viable); slow grind → expect break (no fade) (mech-A #8).
  Read over the approach; a formation-time state feature.
- **STATE-ADVERSE-TOLERANCE** — spatial law, not a predicate: the median winner first
  goes ~18 ticks PAST the touch; defense happens INSIDE the zone (mech-B R2; mech-A #21).
  The accrual window must not disqualify on early adverse excursion, and predicates are
  evaluated on the zone (2-3 tick band), not one price.

## B · Veto / pass predicates (state gates; mostly computable now)

- Effort-REWARDED veto: the push into the extreme held/paid → no reversal read (all lanes).

- The D2 lesson binds here: NONE of these worked as formation-second discriminators in my
  blind test — their hypothesized value is IN THE ACCRUAL (how they evolve after
  formation), which is exactly what the harness below measures.

## D · The test binding (nothing above ships without this)

Preregistered harness (extends D3-accrual, feeds design round 2):
1. Grain: candidate-seconds grouped by series, the D2 pairing frame at scale — ALL
   same-(asset,day,phase) winner/loser series pairs from the round-0 matrix (not 36; all
   of them), plus the ungraded full population for rate curves.
2. For each predicate P and each offset Δ in {0,30,60,120,180,300}s (matrix holds sampled
   offsets to 300s; Δ>300s requires dense-store recomputation via the R6 native engine —
   priced separately before any build): compute P at formation+Δ, measure
   winner-vs-loser separation (AUC / hit-fraction with CIs) and the joint curve
   separation-vs-Δ. Shuffle arm per predicate (within-(asset,day) label permutation).
3. Beside it, D5 prices the oracle cost of deciding at formation+Δ on the SAME Δ grid —
   the pair of curves (information gained vs ceiling lost) IS the design-round-2 input.
4. Promotion bar: a predicate enters the design only with separation clearing its shuffle
   arm at ≥3 of 5 seeds AND surviving the per-asset grain (no pooled-only wins) —
   preregistering-results applies in full; book-quoted numbers (27%, 3-tick, 80%) are
   PRIORS to test, never evidence.
5. Beyond-book mandate: the harness sweeps predicate parameters (tick thresholds, window
   lengths, replenishment counts) around the book's values — if 5 ticks beats 3 on our
   data, ours wins.
