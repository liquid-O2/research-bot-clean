## Sequence (not a score bag)

1. Read what the opposing side has **failed** to do, not the last few candles. Here: strong move already in, sellers making repeated attempts to reclaim the prior range, repeated failure. Weekly delta profile: heavy buying aggression behind the move. That is who is in control of the auction.
2. In a genuine all-time-high environment the objective going in is **neutral**. Wait for the pullback. Trade within the directional push. Do not marry a target before the auction has given you structure.
3. A 350% divergence box may flag. It is not a signal. It means: passive absorption on one side and aggressive pressure on the other, at the same price. Look.
4. Check HTF at that price: was the opposing side already trapped here on the way up? Delta profile: buying aggression getting covered rather than reversed.
5. Enter as origin-of-the-move only once the side that had been rewarded at this level earlier **fails to hold it a second time**.
6. Live trigger on trade 1: price shows the strength to push through a short-term microbalance. Then the long is on.

Waiting is the thesis: "Marrying a bias before the auction has given you structure is exactly the psychological trap this thesis process is built to avoid." (p4)
