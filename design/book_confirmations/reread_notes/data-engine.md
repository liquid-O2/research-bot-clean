## Sequence (not a score bag)

This PDF does not give a tape confirmation. It gives the process that a confirmation is allowed to come from.

1. Pick the concepts (auction, break and retest, liquidity models). Raw material. Nothing more.
2. Turn them into a process: a step-by-step execution model. Test: if two people following your rules would take different trades, it is not a process yet. Do not trade it.
3. Data-collect that process, same way, over many trades.
4. Compare all winners against all losers. The recurring similarities are the actual edge. Refine around those. Repeat.

Journaling is not step 3. "Journaling looks at individual mistakes. Data collection looks at system level behaviour. You need both, but only one of them finds edges." (p3)

## Probabilities, not certainties (p4) — a pass on single-print conviction

You cannot predict the next single candle, any single outcome, the market's next move in isolation.

You can predict what tends to happen across many samples.

Stoic's analogy: you cannot predict what one person does under stress; you can predict how most people react. "Edges live in the aggregate, never in the single trade."

That is a wait/pass law for confirmation design: a one-print "this is the trade" is not licensed. Confirmation is a repeated behaviour at a location.

## Pass / no-trade

- Rules two people would execute differently → not a process, no trade (p3).
- Acting on a single candle as if it were the edge → forbidden (p4).
- Input is a feeling → output is a guess (p5, p9). Trend strength, vs-history, C-scores, standard deviations, macro cycles are how Stoic removed feelings from *fundamentals*, not from tape. Do not import C-scores into our tape features.
- Compounding model (p7–8) requires 100+ trades of data first. That is a launch-sample wait, not a candidate wait. Size is out of scope anyway.
