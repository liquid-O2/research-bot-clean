## Sequence (entry-side)

The PDF is mindset. The only ordered confirmation it gives is the pre-market ritual that decides whether you are allowed to look for a trade at all.

1. Map the auction before the bell. Yesterday's value, the naked POCs, the VIX read, the day-type call. Ten minutes. Walk in with a plan, not a hope.
2. Write the A+ setup down. One sentence. If the trade in front of you does not match it, it is not a trade. The written rule beats the impulse.
3. Then, and only then, the session is allowed to produce an entry. One framework (the auction, the order flow) until mastery. No system hopping.
4. Feel fear / greed / frustration. Act on none of it. The gap between feeling and action is the skill.

Waiting is the method: "If the trade in front of you does not match it, it is not a trade" (p7). Doing nothing is a legal state.

p7 items 3–5 (hard loss limit, journal deviations, close the laptop) are session-stop / conduct. Not entry.

## Pass / no-trade

- Does not match the written one-sentence A+ → not a trade (p7).
- System-hopping / extra tools "because today looks different" → pass. "No system hopping. Stick to one framework, the auction, the order flow, until mastery." (p5)
- Hot-streak overconfidence → stay self-aware; that is when recklessness gets invited (p5). Not a tape pass, a session pass.
- Losing streak while off-plan → step back before the account pays for the lesson (p6). If still on-plan, keep taking valid trades (variance). The discriminator is "were you following the plan?"
- Overmanaging a live trade kills the probabilities the system was built on (p6). That is an anti-tweak rule once in; it also argues against adding extra confirmation filters mid-candidate.
