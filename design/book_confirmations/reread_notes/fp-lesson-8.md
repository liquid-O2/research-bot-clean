## Sequence (not a score bag)

1. Bring the level first. VP ledges and shelves, value edges, old POCs. The footprint is the microscope. The level is the slide. A number without a location is trivia.
2. Read **diagonals into the level**, not side by side. Ask at a price fights the bid **one tick below**. Approach that is exhausted aggression is a different trade from approach that is fresh conviction.
3. Look for **stacked** imbalances at that level. One flagged cell is noise. Two or three in a row is real pressure. Three or more in a column is an unfinished auction the market tends to return and finish.
4. Watch the high-volume prices inside the candle (the candle POC). That row is the magnet on the retest.
5. Pair with the DOM for pace. Footprint is history after the print. DOM is the live rhythm. Together they are the trigger layer.

Waiting for the retest of a stacked-imbalance zone is lawful. Do not enter on a single imbalance in the middle of nowhere.

Stacked 3+ in a column is a zone to mark and **revisit**, which needs G2 plus a level-memory ledger (**G7**) so the unfinished column is still a candidate later.
