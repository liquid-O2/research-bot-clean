## Sequence (not a score bag)

1. Mark the key levels **before** looking at prints. Old highs and lows, ledges, value edges. Same trap locations as the ladder lesson.
2. On approach, get order flow up. The read you are waiting for is **delta against the candle**.
3. Name the disagreement. Bullish absorption: candle up, delta negative (sellers aggressive, price still rises). Bearish absorption: candle down, delta positive (buyers aggressive, price still falls).
4. Interpret the trap: the aggressive side is stuck. Trade with the institutions, against the trapped.
5. Then the **POC flip** in your direction: the busiest price inside the candle jumps to the other side. Control changed hands. At a key level, that flip is confirmation the level is being defended.
6. Delta divergence is the strongest tell and it has two flavours. Regular: price makes a new high/low, delta does not. Exhaustion: huge delta bar at the extreme, price refuses to extend, turn usually one or two candles away.

Stack: **level, then absorption, then flip**. One tell is interest. Two is a trade. Absorption plus flip plus divergence says they are winning. DOM from lesson 6 plus these prints are the same event from two angles. When they agree, execute.

Waiting for the candle to close (footprint proves after the fact) and for the flip/divergence to print is lawful. The DOM sees it live. The footprint is the proof.
