## Sequence (not a score bag)

1. Understand the **current auction** first. Inside balance or already broken. Those two states call for opposite behaviour. Do this before any tape.
2. Stop using **live VAH/VAL as levels**. They recalculate as volume prints. Real extremes are ledges and prior-balance POCs that stay put.
3. Day type is a layer, not an entry. P/B already resolved: do not fade the winning side. D is compact, both edges, no resolution yet. Fine to wait. Re-read after every impulse.
4. Overnight, 6pm to 9:30 NY: net long or net short. That bias carries into the open until the overnight **LVN or shelf** is disrespected. One clean distribution: environment continues. Double distribution: the LVN between the humps is the decision level.
5. STAT-94 is a **wait**, not a go: 94% chance RTH tags ONH **or** ONL, not both. A long a few points above ONL with the stop under it is the wrong time, not the wrong level. Wait for the nearer overnight extreme.
6. The Failed Auction **setup** (narrow): established balance, break out of it, travel to a **prior/older balance's POC**, **instant reject**. Target is the established balance's own VAH (reject from above) or VAL (reject from below). Not any retest. Not acceptance at that POC.
7. Loose 80% rule (research-grade, p18): open outside previous day's value, then hold back inside for **two consecutive 30-minute periods**, then 80% chance of a full traverse. Confirmation matures on those two periods. Waiting is the rule.
8. Tape at the tagged POC or the re-entered edge. This book does not supply the trigger. AMT/VP is still the where.

HTF outranks LTF (p13). A clean 15-second short at the lower edge of a higher-timeframe balance gets **less** leniency, not more. The auction context outranks the trigger.

## Feature mapping or named gap

`disc_prior_*` covers prior-session POC and ON H/L (STAT-94 is computable now). `disc_ib_*` covers IB breaks (p23: at least one IB side breaks 96-99% of ES days, so an IB-edge "hold" is almost never the trade). `disc_auction_*` covers inside/outside value. `disc_level_z*` / `disc_evt_*` cover instant rejection at a tagged price.

Named gap **G8**: older **multi-day** balance registry (the F/A setup needs a prior balance's POC, not yesterday's only), overnight LVN / double-distribution / shelf, MPOC (profile mid, not POC). Without that registry the quiz on p11 cannot be asked.

Two consecutive 30-minute holds inside prior value (p18) is a clock on 30-minute brackets. Derivable from timestamps. Not a current feature.
