This is the confirmation grammar for aggression bubbles. Two trades, one pattern, decided by regime *before* the chart: short-gamma origin-of-the-move (rare), long-gamma fade-the-extreme (the other 80%).

## Sequence (not a score bag)

**Before a single bubble:**
1. Name the regime: in balance or out of it, short gamma or long gamma.
2. Aggression threshold is instrument-specific, not borrowed. On NQ he runs 30–60 contracts on a 40-range chart, from his own data collection.
3. Treat a bubble as effort, never as a buy/sell. Price is whether the effort was rewarded.

**Reading the print:**
4. Body vs wick, made consciously. Aggression inside the candle body = that side was willing to trade at worse prices and got carried. Same print on the wick = they pushed and were absorbed.
5. If both sides are being absorbed, nobody is in control. Wait for the break. Do not pick a side inside the range.
6. An imbalance line (350% more of one side at a single price) only matters where it sits on the same price as aggression.

**Origin of the move, short-gamma only:**
7. A squeeze is a sequence, not a candle: aggressive buyers print in the body (normally "paired") and price goes lower anyway. Repeated. Those buyers are exhausted. That is fuel, not a trigger.
8. Do **not** enter on the failure of the squeeze. That is where the previous attempt died. Nothing has confirmed a new attempt is starting.
9. Enter the **drive**: price takes out the wicks above (buyers now willing to pay higher). Resting liquidity above converts. Combined with fresh aggressive buyers at the retest.
10. The refill area below must hold: sellers previously failed there; they refill; buyers absorb that refill instead of breaking down. That is confirmation.
11. Take the **retest** of the break, not the break. Firing on the break is "prefiring": firing on the expectation of confirmation. Some of these never give the retest back. He lets those go. Waiting is lawful.
12. Higher timeframe must agree: current auction was balancing, price left, support is a minor volume node at the extreme of that balance, nothing underneath (no participation if it goes there). Failed-auction long: balance → out of balance → reject at prior value.
13. CVD is not sitting against you on the timeframe you are entering from.

**The other 80% (long gamma / in balance):**
14. Do not wait for a squeeze. A balanced market has no reason to produce one.
15. Fade the extreme on failure of aggression: buyers absorbed at the top, they are still not rewarded on the return, enter the test back, target where the other side last had control. You no longer need your own side to be rewarded. Passive absorption is the mechanism.

## Page 5 figure — imbalance + aggression at the same price

Caption: "SELL AGGRESSION AND A SELL IMBALANCE AT THE SAME PRICE."

Grey box at a price row with a pink bubble on it. A short green vertical links the box to the print. Price then walks lower. More pink bubbles down the drop. Red imbalance lines stacked.

Rule the figure encodes: mark the level where large *and* one-sided prints sit together. Sellers accepting worse (lower) fills means they are trying to get size done, not a good price. That is why he waits for price to come back and retest rather than chasing the print.

## Page 13 figure — passive move

A green long is working. The up-leg has almost no white (buy) bubbles. Caption: "A MOVE THAT WENT THE RIGHT WAY WITH NO AGGRESSION BEHIND IT."

Sequence he wants instead, in order: (1) opposition's aggression fails (exhausted / trapped / absorbed), (2) *then* your side arrives aggressively. Opposition failing on its own is only half. Half is what most people trade.

Pass: price going your way with nothing behind it is a short-term swing, not the start of the drive. Do not trail a confirmation you do not have.

## Checklist (p18) translated to entry confirmation

Before a bubble: regime (balance vs not, gamma), instrument threshold, effort vs reward.

Reading: body vs wick called; both-sides-absorbed → wait for the break; imbalance only with aggression at the same price.

OFM short-gamma: repeated unrewarded effort; enter the drive not the squeeze death; wicks above taken and refill below held; on the retest (no retest → let it go); HTF agrees (left balance, thin behind you, level that should reject); CVD not against you.

Balance-day: extreme with failed-to-get-paid aggression; enter the test back; do not wait for a squeeze.

Last two boxes are size ("is my side being rewarded" is entry; "does RR fit the account" is out of scope).

## Pass / no-trade

- Bubble used as a buy/sell signal → pass. It shows orders. Nothing else.
- Both sides absorbed → wait for the break.
- Entering the squeeze failure → pass. That is the misread the title of p7 exists to kill.
- Prefiring the break → pass. No retest → miss it. He accepts that.
- CVD against you on the entry timeframe → pass (p12, p18).
- HTF still balancing with no thin-behind-you asymmetry → OFM drive is not licensed.
- Long-gamma / in-balance → do not take the OFM drive even if the LTF pattern rhymes. Same pattern, different trade.
- Passive move in your direction → do not treat it as the drive.
- Waiting for a squeeze on a balance day → pass. You will talk yourself out of the 80% trade.

p17 (prop-firm maths, high-RR skew) is account structure. Out of scope except the regime point: you cannot build a month around the rare A++. The framework has to answer the 80% case too.

## Feature mapping

- Body vs wick of the aggression print → approximate from price-at-print vs bar range. Partial. Not a named gap if path is 1s; it is if we only have OHLC.
- 350% per-price imbalance → **G2**. Named gap. Dawes cannot see "350% more sellers than buyers at this price row."
- Aggression bubbles at 30–60 on NQ 40-range → `disc_evt_*` size threshold. Computable *if* we pick an instrument-specific band from data, which the PDF requires. Copying 30–60 onto HG/NKD/SI is a miss.
- Squeeze = repeated one-side `disc_evt_*` with non-positive same-direction displacement. Computable.
- Drive = wick take-out + refill-hold + retest. `disc_level_z*` / rolling high cross + refill (`disc_evt_reloads` / `disc_quote_*`). Sequence, not an average.
- HTF minor node + thin below → **G8** profile-node detection. Named gap.
- Gamma regime (short vs long) → **G9**. Named gap. Same chart pattern is two trades; without G9 we will fire OFM on the 80% days.
- CVD vs its own median → **G10**. Named gap. p12 figure is the existence proof.
- Level already defended once (retest vs prefire) → **G7**. Named gap.
