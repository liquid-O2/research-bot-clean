## Sequence (not a score bag)

1. Location already exists: a balance redrawn until it actually fits price. Ill-fitting balance is a reason to redraw, not a reason to force the read.
2. Ask: is the extreme **finished**? Price has already run into the upper extreme. By AMT it is expected to reject, not extend. One failed push is noise. Two failed pushes at the same price, in two different sessions, is the market saying where it is not willing to trade.
3. Ask: who owns the Delta at that high. Heavy one-sided **buying** into the high is the fuel for the short, not a reason to join the buyers. Buyers who buy a high and then watch price turn are forced sellers. G1 is how you see that print as a price row.
4. Wait twice. Wait for a breakout of the **intraday** range. Wait again for price to come back and retest it. If the retest does not come, stand aside and look higher, at the same daily extreme. Two waits, not one.
5. Only then: short on the retest of a level that has already been defended. Tape has to show aggressive selling in the candle **bodies** (not sitting passively on the offer) bar after bar, or the idea is still a hope.

Target sizing and the $501.50 fill are out of scope. The entry is the retest after the analysis was already done.

## Page 11 (no figure)

Six things, all checked **before** the entry:
- a balance corrected until it actually fit price
- an extreme of that balance, reached and not exceeded
- a Delta print at that extreme read as trapped buyers rather than strength
- two independent sessions failing to break the same level
- an intraday breakout, followed by the retest he waited for rather than chased

None of those six alone is a signal. Together, in the order they showed up, they are the case.

## Page 12 checklist (entry-side)

BEFORE THE SESSION
- My balance actually fits price. If it doesn't, I redraw it before trading off it.
- I know where price sits relative to the extremes of that balance.

READING THE DELTA
- Heavy one sided volume at an extreme is a flag for trapped positioning, not automatically a signal in that direction.
- I've checked whether a prior session already failed at the same level.

THE ENTRY
- I have a breakout AND a retest, not just the breakout.
- The tape itself is showing aggression in my direction before I size the trade normally.

SIZING THE TARGET (out of scope as a goal; listed so it is not silently imported)
- I've decided whether this session's normal range historically runs bigger than my target, and sized accordingly on purpose.
- My take profit does not depend on above average movement showing up to be worth taking.

## What we implemented wrong

We treated heavy buy Delta at a high as a long feature. This PDF treats that print as trapped fuel for the short, and only after the extreme has failed twice and the intraday break has been retested. G1 is the trapped-buyers row. The wait is the second wait (retest), not the break. Tape-in-the-body is the last gate, not a separate score.
