## Sequence (not a score bag)

1. Before the open, read the **number and the curve**, not as a click. VIX is weather. Expected daily move = VIX / sqrt(252). Contango = trade the levels. Backwardation = the auction is broken until the curve normalises. Rising VVIX is the early warning that a quiet VIX is about to get loud.
2. Match the **auction layer** to that weather. Low VIX: rotation, respect value, scalps or stand down. Mid VIX (~16): structured continuation and opening-drive plays are in season. High VIX: expansion, do not fade strength.
3. Intraday, read **direction of VIX with ES**, not the level alone. ES up + VIX down = continuation. ES up + VIX up = the rally may fade. ES down + VIX up = do not catch the knife. Flat/flat = chop.
4. Rising VIX intraday: expect ES to push **away from value**. Falling VIX: expect rotation and respect of VAH/VAL.
5. On event days, ignore the implied range. Pre-event VIX rising: do not over-fade. Post vol-crush: back to fading rotations. The trap is trading yesterday's regime.
6. Then the tape at the AMT/VP level. VIX never tells you to click.

Size and stop width are out of scope. What this lesson licenses for **entry** is which layer is even allowed: fade the value edges, or only take continuation.

Waiting is the method on a VIX-below-13 day and once the implied range is mostly spent.

## Auction link (p9), the load-bearing sequence

- Balance on the profile **and VIX dropping**: fade value edges toward POC. Rotation is the play.
- Balance on the profile **and VIX rising**: do not lean on the walls. Expansion is loading.
- Breakout attempts while VIX is dropping hard fail into rotation more often. Real breaks need real vol behind them.

That is AMT location first, VIX as the allow/deny on the fade-versus-break layer, tape last.
