# Entry V2 execution path

## Components found

- Native candidate formation starts at `qr_entry_v2_g1` in `engine/cpp/qr_entry_v2/tools/qr_entry_v2_g1.cpp:124-269`. The `candidates` stage calls `build_g1_candidate_artifacts`; `teacher` is a separate privileged stage. `CandidateRow` in `engine/cpp/qr_entry_v2/include/qr_entry_v2/g1.hpp:164-229` has no outcome fields. It carries session and lock identity, zigzag confirmation time and ordinal, later decision time, side, phase, rung mask, delay kind, phase clocks, strict prefix cutoff and hashes, entry BBO and cost, prior ATR and spread, and compliance status.
- The raw formation algorithm is `generate_g1_candidates` in `engine/cpp/qr_entry_v2/src/g1.cpp:859-1169`. Four zigzag machines use thresholds derived from strictly prior ATR14 and spread priors. They scan `QRE2EVT2` rows in receive order, reset on generation changes, and accept only trusted, economic, two-sided, sane rows. A reversal creates a confirmation. A native candidate decision is later, at confirmation plus 120 seconds, or plus 15 seconds for the fast-open duplicate. The entry BBO is the last sane BBO strictly before that decision. Equal-receive-time rows stay future.
- Formation outcomes stay on the teacher plane. `certify_teacher` in `engine/cpp/qr_entry_v2/src/g1.cpp:1200-1364` begins at the candidate's strict cutoff and scans only the suffix for wall, close, MFE, and MAE. This is label generation, not an observation source.
- The original corpus entry point is `build_corpus` in `engine/entry_v2/corpus.py:1505-2410`. It strict-loads candidate, teacher, and event manifests, verifies candidate and teacher identity sets, rechecks every strict prefix cutoff and hash, joins forecast and causal context at the candidate decision clock, and constructs `CausalEntryExample` separately from `ReplayOutcome`. Eligible empty and outage sessions remain denominator rows rather than disappearing.
- Recovery-corpus discovery is `discover_authoritative_session_specs` in `engine/entry_v2/confirmation_experiment.py:250-389`. It binds each session to the pinned G1 candidate and teacher manifests, event pack, forecast artifact and receipt, and strictly prior session, and refuses the sealed half. `materialize_outcome_corpus` in `engine/entry_v2/tabular_campaign.py:251-285`, `materialize_teacher_corpus` at `:603-702`, and `materialize_feature_corpus` at `:790-860` are the restartable corpus stages.
- Short confirmation is `materialize_confirmation_session` in `engine/entry_v2/confirmation.py:1655-1958`. `ConfirmationConfig` and its receipt are in `confirmation.py:139-217`. A row is scheduled from `ceil(candidate.decision_ts_ns)` and is valid only through the configured watch and before phase close. `searchsorted(..., side="left")` fixes `event_cutoff`; `_OutcomeIndex.current` returns the last trusted BBO before the snapshot. The row receipt binds the stream receipt, formation parity, config, series, snapshot, cutoff, prefix census, active candidate IDs, forecast lineage, prior-session receipt, and slow-context row hash.
- Causal feature construction is `_SessionPlane.feature_map` in `engine/entry_v2/confirmation.py:1200-1385`. It exposes the current trusted book, age, phase time remaining, movement from formation, frozen formation facts, candidate rung and delay facts, discretionary prefix state, and prefix windows of 1, 5, 15, 30, 60, 120, 300, 600, and 1,800 seconds. Every window ends at the snapshot. `compute_outcomes=False` prevents suffix access and leaves typed placeholders that are stripped at the feature boundary.
- Dense labels and sparse observations are different durable interfaces. `DelayedOutcomeShard` in `engine/entry_v2/tabular_delayed_corpus.py:71-268` is `QRE2TABOUTCOME1` and may contain future outcome fields. `CausalFeatureShard` in `tabular_delayed_corpus.py:271-416` is `QRE2TABFEATURESHARD2`; its validator rejects malformed prefixes and its feature-name validator rejects outcome-shaped names. `materialize_delayed_outcome_session` in `tabular_delayed_corpus.py:448-590` recomputes entry BBO, cost, wall, exit, and PnL at every resolvable receive-second. It does not build model features.
- `DayOptionUniverse.from_shards` in `engine/entry_v2/exact_delayed_teacher.py:145-205` joins the three asset outcome shards for one day. `ExactDaySolver` in `exact_delayed_teacher.py:345-812` applies one option per series, one closed-interval position per asset, and the shared 12-entry daily cap. `build_exact_delayed_teacher_day` in `exact_delayed_teacher.py:1128-1244` publishes `QRE2EXACTDELAYEDTEACHER2` with the exact schedule, component targets, and ENTER/DEFER/PASS suffix values. Realized exit time stays in the suffix solver; the published active-position clock is scheduled phase close.
- The current causal policy walk is `replay_policy_day` in `engine/entry_v2/tabular_live_replay.py:296-568`. It combines every-second `CausalFeatureShard` rows into exact timestamp batches, predicts components from the causal row, builds `PortfolioDecisionState`, chooses ENTER/DEFER/PASS, resolves simultaneous ENTER proposals per asset and remaining portfolio seats, and emits `PolicyDayTrace` under `QRE2TABLIVEREPLAY3`. Its receipt binds selected opportunity IDs, exact commit arrivals, proposals, threshold crossings, action changes, models, feature schema, outcome universe, and feature shard receipts.
- Canonical chronological accounting is `replay` in `engine/entry_v2/replay.py:290-453`. It sorts by exact nanosecond and candidate ID. Only equal nanoseconds are simultaneous. Occupancy blocks an asset while `open_until >= decision_ts_ns`, so an exit must be strictly earlier than the next entry. It enforces the shared 12-entry cap, preserves zero-entry asset-days through `expected_sessions`, and computes trade-path results and drawdown. `replay_policy_block` in `tabular_live_replay.py:569-613` is the connected policy-to-replay adapter. `replay_exact_teacher_day` in `exact_delayed_teacher.py:1377-1414` proves the exact teacher schedule has cent parity through the same replay.
- The new-extreme branch is diagnostic, not part of that connected path. `extreme_events` in `tools/probe_extreme_events.py:89-125` sorts each cell by `formed + 180`, then marks a row only when its own-side score strictly beats the running record. The first eligible row per side is always an event. `probe_extreme_events.run` at `:229-390` reads only age 180 plus formation age 0. `probe_location_ranker.run` in `tools/probe_location_ranker.py:154-329` uses the same rows and TRAIN-frozen level features. Both eventually use `_best_by_score_per_cell`, whose own contract at `probe_extreme_events.py:207-216` says it is not causal on its own because it chooses the best event after seeing the cell's complete event set. Their `_cash_stats` and `_cell_pick` occupancy path is diagnostic and does not call canonical `replay`.

## Flow

1. `qr_entry_v2_g1 --stage candidates` reads the locked raw event pack and prior-only session statistics. Zigzag reversal confirmation is the first clock. The native candidate's formation or alert clock used by Python is its later `decision_ts_ns`, after the fixed 15- or 120-second delay. These clocks must not be conflated.
2. At the native decision, the row knows only the strict receive prefix, last prior trusted BBO, side and phase, rung and delay, scheduled phase close, prior ATR14, prior spread, and lineage. It does not know whether the reversal pays, its later path, wall time, or exit.
3. `build_corpus` validates that formation row and keeps its teacher only as target/replay data. The recovery lane instead calls `materialize_delayed_outcome_session` for dense privileged labels and `materialize_confirmation_session(..., compute_outcomes=False)` for causal observations. The split is structural, not a convention.
4. Short-confirmation age is measured from the native candidate decision, not from zigzag confirmation. Because snapshots start at the next whole receive-second, a nominal age 0 row usually has a positive fractional `min_alert_age_sec`. At every later age, the row adds only events whose receive availability is strictly before the snapshot and recomputes entry BBO and cost at that snapshot.

| Supported schedule | Nominal ages | What the row knows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `CORPUS` | 0, 30, 60, 90, 120, 180, 240, 290, 300 | Formation facts, current prior BBO and cost, elapsed age, scheduled phase time, active watch counts, strict-prefix market and discretionary windows, decision-time forecast lineage, strict-prior session and slow context. |
| `TRAINING/FULL`, 300 | 0 through 60 every 5 seconds, then 70 through 300 every 10 seconds | The same schema, evaluated on the longer observed prefix. The label plane recomputes suffix PnL from that snapshot BBO and charges cost once. |
| `TRAINING/FULL`, 600 | The 300-second grid plus 330 through 600 every 30 seconds | The same causal schema. No implementation path accepts a delay above 600 seconds. |
| `REPLAY`, 300 or 600 | Every integer second from 0 through the configured maximum | Runtime observation sequence used for chronological policy decisions. Outcome fields are absent from `CausalFeatureShard`. |
| Existing accrual probe | 0, 30, 60, 120, 180, 240, 290 | Diagnostic comparison in `tools/probe_trained_accrual.py:79-285`; it omits corpus ages 90 and 300. |
| Existing event and location probes | 0 for formation bookkeeping and 180 for exact event eligibility and entry label | Event score, side, cell, formation clock, location geometry, and exact age-180 target. No other commit age is tested by these probes. |

5. A causal event process may update the running own-side extreme when each row reaches age 180. That operation uses only the row being admitted and earlier admitted events. A live selector may ENTER, DEFER, or PASS at that same timestamp using its age-180 features and current portfolio state. It may not use `_best_by_score_per_cell` across the finished cell, the best final event, event-oracle `y`, or a phase-end event count.
6. In the connected tabular path, `replay_policy_day` visits observations in timestamp order. It removes a position only after its exit has occurred, exposes scheduled phase close while the position is active, carries consumed and passed watches, and caps simultaneous decisions by asset occupancy and remaining seats. Selected rows become `ScoredArrival` objects with the policy's exact snapshot as `decision_ts_ns`; outcome fields are attached only for accounting.
7. `replay_policy_block` concatenates those committed arrivals and calls `replay`. Thus formation time determines when a watch begins, observation time determines what may be known, commit time determines entry BBO, outcome and occupancy interval, and exact replay time. A late-confirmation experiment must preserve all four clocks in its receipt.

Time-valid information can enter at four existing gates without exposing outcomes. Raw market messages enter through the strict receive cutoff. Forecast features enter through `ForecastQuery` at the native candidate decision, with lineage bound in every later row. Prior-session facts require a strictly earlier trading day. Slow context enters through `tabular_context_summary` at scheduled snapshots and is receipt-bound per row. Current portfolio state may include entries already committed, positions whose exit has already happened, scheduled phase closes, consumed or passed watches, and seats used. Future wall time, future close PnL, best later event, final event ordinal, and exact oracle schedule remain label-plane data.

## Non-obvious things

- “Formation” currently names two different clocks. C++ records zigzag reversal confirmation, then emits a tradable native decision 15 or 120 seconds later. Python confirmation age zero starts after that native decision. Any recovery receipt should name both clocks rather than overload `formation_ts`.
- Event eligibility at 180 seconds is causal. Selecting the highest-ranked event across the completed cell is not. The current location result therefore establishes an outcome-bearing event roster and an offline ranking ceiling, not a runnable stopping rule.
- The event probes' occupancy is a diagnostic approximation. `_cell_pick` in `tools/probe_trained_accrual.py:217-285` serializes entries within one asset using supplied occupancy, but it does not enforce the shared 12-entry portfolio cap and does not call `replay`.
- The current Python replay law is one position per asset plus 12 shared entries. The native G1 schedule constants in `g1.hpp:24-42` still describe the older three-per-asset and nine-per-portfolio ceiling. A recovery design should not inherit those native caps by accident.
- The planned ages 1,200 through 10,800 seconds do not exist in source. `ConfirmationConfig`, `training_offsets_seconds`, `replay_offsets_seconds`, and `DelayedOutcomeShard.validate` accept only 300 or 600 seconds. The plan's 600, 1,200, 2,400, 3,600, 5,400, 7,200, and 10,800-second tail is therefore a proposed extension, not an available corpus.
- A `ConfirmationDataset` can physically contain causal features and outcomes together for training, but `CausalFeatureShard` is the live model boundary. Runtime materialization requires `compute_outcomes=False`, checks the outcome-mode receipt, and strips the placeholders. Review and tests should target this boundary rather than assume every confirmation dataset is safe to hand to a policy.
- The policy walk uses realized exit only to release occupancy after the exit timestamp has passed. The action feature state receives scheduled phase close while the position is active. This models information becoming known over time without revealing a future wall-hit clock.

## Open questions

- Which causal stopping rule replaces `_best_by_score_per_cell` for new-extreme events? The repository has event formation and offline rankers, but no event-specific ENTER/DEFER/PASS adapter into `replay_policy_day` or canonical `replay`.
- Should a late-tail implementation extend the existing dense outcome and causal feature contracts beyond 600 seconds, or materialize a separate sparse tail? Source currently refuses the planned ages, so protocol freeze must precede that write.
- Should `replay_policy_day` receive a small injected decision interface over `PortfolioDecisionState`? That is the narrowest change that would let formation-only, fixed-180, and later-confirmation policies consume the same observation sequence and emit comparable commit clocks without duplicating occupancy or replay law.
- The original `build_corpus` and the recovery lane's delayed-outcome plus causal-feature corpus are both authoritative in different tickets. The recovery plan should name which one owns the 2022 through 2024 launch receipt before any build starts.
