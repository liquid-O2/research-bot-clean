reversal extreme is formed, each mapped to a column we already have or a

## The grammar (order, not average)

Stages must happen at the **same 2–3 tick zone**, in order. A later stage
without the earlier ones is noise. The zone is not one price
(`dom-lesson-6` p4: 300 contracts can be eaten across 3 ticks).

**S0. Already true before any print means anything.**
Location is a finished extreme (shelf, ledge, LVN, mVN, prior-day VAH/VAL),
never POC, never mid-balance, never a still-moving current-day VAH/VAL
(`mastering-amt-vp`: stop using live VAH/VAL as levels). Memory: has this
zone been defended before, today or a prior session. Thesis: AMT gives
where, never when. Arrival: aggressive into the extreme expects defense;
slow grind expects break. `whos-in-control` p4: "the extreme by itself
decides nothing." `code-3-orderflow` p6: inside a balance is a wait, not a
smaller trade. `average-unprofitable-trader` p27–30: Wait is a drawn pill
on the flow map. Confluence count is not a step (F9).

**S1. Effort arrives and is not paid.** Aggression hits, price does not go.
This forms the candidate. It is not confirmation. `18k` p11 and
`dom-lesson-6` p3: a single absorption print is never enough.
Discriminator inside S1 (`dom-lesson-6` p7): big volume with no movement is
absorption (someone is there, reversal licensed later). Shrinking volume
with no movement is exhaustion (nobody left, a pause). Both look like "no
displacement." Only one is a start.

**S2. Defense replenishes while it is still being hit, and prints trade
through it.** Iceberg replenishes as it trades. Spoof vanishes before it
trades (`dom-lesson-7` p5, p7). Reload is real, cancel is fake. Ratio:
rebuild over traded volume at that price. 1–2 tick replenishment is the
named fake-out zone (`stop-re-entering` p10). The 18k tell is the *trend
across the 2nd and 3rd refresh*, not the first reprint (p11).

**S3. The attacker decays.** Prints thin, tape slows, delta turns against
them. Digit read: doubles and triples shrink to singles on the losing side
(`stop-re-entering` p12).

**S4. The other side arrives aggressively.** Not the absence of the first
side. A second energy source. `your-mistakes-with-absorption` p13.
`stop-re-entering` p12: "Passive absorption is the setup; aggression is
the trigger." `only-trade-big-trades` p13: trading the first half alone
is the common error.

**S5. A small immediate reward.** Book prior: ~3 ticks toward the trade
(their 27% fail-without-it number, their instrument), or two to four
upticks (`stop-re-entering` p12). A delayed reward is treated as no
reward. Entry sits within a tick or two of that confirmation. Six to
eight ticks late means there is probably no reward system (p13–14).

**S6. Price returns, and the same side defends again. This is the entry.**
Every figure that draws an ENTRY arrow puts it on the retest after
confirming aggression, never on the wall, never on the first print.
`18k` p7, first-hand: "A level does not become the trade because price
touched it. It becomes the trade when the same side defends it a second
time, with more conviction than the first." First two attempts at that
exact level had no participation and were let go. Third attempt: "I'm
in." The licensed short sits on the OFM retest from below, not in Trade
2's white box at the high. `trapped-buyers-one-retest` p6: two waits,
planned in advance. `origin-of-the-move` p3: let the first squeeze fail,
enter on the re-squeeze. `anatomy-of-a-losing-start` p7: the only lawful
re-entry is a return to the **same** band. `reading-delta` p4, first-hand:
ENTRY on a white dashed line after the low is in; stamp "ONLY DO THIS
METHOD WITH CONFIRMED LOWS / FINISHED AUCTIONS." `stop-re-entering` cover:
five red X's on longs into the drop, then a ring-and-dot labeled
CONFIRMED on the first teal candle after the low, not on the wick.

Defense happens **inside** the zone. Acting at the first tag is acting where
the refill paper's own data says the trade has not started. The distance of
that dip is a measured quantity on their instrument, not a constant we copy.
See Scale vs order.

The 18k pre-file shorts (Trades 1 and 2) are the book pricing the
unconfirmed state at a small known negative (−$100, −$40). They are not a
conviction recipe. Copying first-touch as an entry is copying the loss.

The clock is event-indexed: nth refresh, second test, candle close, ticks
past the level. The one hard clock in the corpus is a 30-minute cancel on
an unfilled rest (`refill-effect` p12). 300 s was a guess. It sits in the
middle of their observed waits, not at the top. Fastest end is live ladder
(seconds). 18k's licensed fill is several 40-range bars, ~4–5 minutes of
clock at the NY open. Slow end is session CVD.

B-set does not add stages. It fills S0: day type, overnight bias, 80% rule
(two consecutive 30-minute holds back inside prior value), STAT-94 as a
wait not a go, VWAP ±2 as a location not a click, VIX as weather for which
layer is allowed. Footprint lessons (fp-8/9) are the same machine from the
other window: stacked imbalance at a level, then POC flip, then close.

## Two encodings (design it twice)

Caller's usage, written first:

```
for each candidate at a snapshot:
    if location is not a finished extreme: skip
    if the ordered stages have not all fired at this zone: skip
    decision_ts = timestamp of the second defense
    take y at decision_ts
```

**Encoding A, taken, with two defects named.** Sequence machine.
Eligibility is order plus defense, not the price-path latches alone.

The `_seen` / `_age_sec` block cannot carry order. The four flags are
nested latches (`discretionary_features.py` 1999–2012): `retest_seen == 1`
already implies lift, reclaim, adverse, and `retest_age_sec < lift_age_sec`.
Ticket 08's old snippet reduced to one boolean. `disc_path_ofm_retest_complete`
is that conjunction and is **absent** from matrix 7e9e2588…;
`disc_path_defended_retest_current` is present and is three displacement
comparisons under a name that says "defended" (2582–2585). S6 is
`retest_seen` joined to quote rebuild-after-depletion and memory
defense-reload (columns verified in the manifest). Snapshot at stored Δ
rows; do not trust `_seen` at an exact transition (`searchsorted left - 1`
returns the prior index, 2527–2528; `state_age` is 0.0 for both never and
just-now, 2531–2533). The state series stops at formation + 601 s (1978).
Keep Δ as a reporting axis so old curves stay comparable.

**Encoding B, rejected.** Dawes bag at a later Δ. Average DEFENSE /
REPLENISH / EXHAUST / LIFTOFF at one second, then wait longer. Already
measured: AUC ~0.60, $200–650/asset-day. A name with high LIFTOFF and no
DEFENSE scores the same as one that ran the order. That is the
implementation failure. `average-unprofitable-trader` F9 is this encoding
under another name: stacking unmeasured confluences.

**Encoding C, held.** Hard S6 gate, then rank the survivors by location
and memory. Same eligibility as A, then a cheap ranker among the few that
completed. Only opens if 07 says timing *and* more than one S6-complete
name remains in the cell. Do not build it before that receipt.

The book's own causal mechanical OFM entry, peeks stripped, is negative
(−0.16R to −0.54R, `origin-of-the-move` p18). Thesis is not optional.
Do not copy a hand-thresholded OFM as the live object.

## Scale vs order

User ruling, this turn: two ticks, three ticks, 18 ticks, and the rest of
the printed distances are arbitrary for us. They have to be calculated on
SI, HG, and NKD. They can move across regimes, days, assets, and years.

The refill paper already says this on its own instrument. p10: "the median
eventual winner first dips 18 ticks past the touch" is a measured NQ/MNQ
median, not a law. p12 then deploys a different number, 12 ticks inside,
as the resting depth. p14: Q4, vol compressed, lost money at every
parameter setting they tried. "The edge is regime-dependent." Copying 18
onto silver is copying a number that already failed a quieter NQ quarter.

Our ticks are not theirs. `ASSET_RAW_TICK` / `ASSET_MULTIPLIER` in
`engine/entry_v2/corpus.py`: SI $25/tick, HG $12.50/tick, NKD $25/tick.
Eighteen NQ ticks is about $90. Eighteen SI ticks is $450. Same integer,
different trade.

**Order stays. Scale is estimated.**

Order, portable, not a distance: location first, unpaid effort, reload with
prints vs vanish, attacker decay, opposite aggression, a reward that is
immediate, second defense of the same zone. Event counts stay integers:
second test, 2nd and 3rd refresh, candle close. DOM 7 p5: "an iceberg
REPLENISHES as it trades, a spoof VANISHES before it trades." That is
behaviour, not a tick count.

Scale, estimated, never copied:

| Quantity the book printed | What we actually measure |
|---|---|
| Zone width ("3 ticks", DOM 6 p4) | Width of the price rows that share one defense event |
| Inside-zone dip ("18 ticks", refill p10) | First-touch to extreme MAE among cell-oracle winners |
| Resting depth ("12 ticks inside", refill p12) | TRAIN quantile of that MAE, if we ever rest. Not an exit recipe |
| Replenishment run ("≥3, 1–2 fake", stop-re-entering p10) | Consecutive ticks at the level with reload, winners vs losers |
| Reward ("3 ticks" / "2–4 upticks") | Displacement after lift-off, in ticks and $ |
| Proximity vs late ("1–2 vs 6–8") | Distance from the lift-off event to fill |
| Imbalance flag ("350%") | Ask/bid ratio at the row. Our own distribution, not 3.5 |
| Fail rate ("27%") | Our fail rate at our own scale, not theirs |

Units on every receipt: own ticks, dollars, and fraction of a recent range
(session ATR or the cell's own span). Dollars are the only unit that
compares across SI/HG/NKD. Ticks are the unit the tape uses. ATR is the
unit that moves with regime.

**Grain, taken.** Estimate per asset × prior evaluation block (half-year,
the grain 07 already uses). Knobs from prior blocks only (D-095). A
day-level quantile is too noisy to be a knob; the day is the evaluation
unit. Split further by a coarse regime already in the matrix (day type,
overnight bias, VIX tercile) only if the asset×block histogram of that
quantity is bimodal enough that one quantile is a lie. Prove the split on
TRAIN. Do not invent a new regime model for this.

**Degenerate, typed.** A quantile that selects nobody or everybody is a
gate defect, not a finding (encoding-goals-in-gates). Shuffle of the
scale parameter (use another asset's quantile, or a draw from the loser
distribution) must be able to fail the comparison.

**Not a selector.** The occupancy of S6 and these scale distributions can
run on the frozen 2021 matrix with no fit. They do not change 07's piles.
If 07 says timing, ticket 08 uses TRAIN quantiles from this measurement,
never 18.

Ticket: `tickets/09-scale-calibration.md`. No blocker. No fit.

## How we learn from this

1. **Do not average stages.** `tools/probe_confirmation_accrual.py:169` is
   `out["COMBINED"] = np.nanmean(...)` over DEFENSE / REPLENISH / EXHAUST /
   LIFTOFF read at the same second. Verified this turn.

2. **Use the transition log we already emit, but not as order.** The
   matrix carries `disc_state_{adverse,reclaim,lift,retest,invalidated}_seen`
   and `_age_sec` for the first four
   (`engine/entry_v2/discretionary_features.py` 2538–2546). There is **no**
   `disc_state_invalidated_age_sec`. Comparing those ages is not order: the
   latches nest, so `retest_seen` implies the rest. Order and defense live
   in the quote/event/memory ledgers (`disc_quote_h30_rebuild_after_depletion_count`,
   `disc_memory_z2_defense_reload_count`, `disc_evt_h30_attack_reload_lift_ordered`).
   Join those to the geometry flag. Do not ask the latches what order
   things happened in.

3. **Do not reopen the closed retest as price geometry.**
   `tools/probe_retest_rule.py` qualifies on running-max extension within
   eps, with no requirement that the level was defended, or defended again
   with more conviction. That null correctly closes "re-test of a held
   price extreme at these grids." It does not close S6 (second defense).
   The defense-rate columns (`disc_test_response_h5_defense_rate`,
   `disc_memory_z*_defense_reload_count`) were never in that probe.

4. **Rebuild vs prints is a ratio, not two +1 features.** In the Dawes
   REPLENISH score, rebuild size and depletion size both carry sign +1, so
   an iceberg and a spoof add up the same. The lesson is rebuild / traded
   volume at the level. Both columns exist.

5. **Ticket 07 runs, amended, and does not gate 08.** The book still
   never ranks 64 near-duplicates; that is a prior, not a sum-to-ceiling
   clause. `ceiling_series_best / ceiling_180` is already 1.06–1.11 on
   every asset (stored-grid timing ≤ $160–$250/asset-day). 07 publishes
   marginal dollars on top of the measured ρ ≈ 0.15 picker. Its letter
   labels which pile 08's dollars sit in. It does not open or close 08.
   The exhausted branch in the diagnosis fires on 08's THRESHOLD+FORWARD
   dollars, whatever letter 07 printed.

6. **If 10 licenses 08, snapshot at stored Δ rows, not a rebuilt
   transition grid.** Six stage timestamps stay fog until 07 says the
   corpus must keep a state stream. Ticket 08 is
   `tickets/08-confirmation-sequence.md`, blocked by 10. Receipt path:
   `artifacts/entry_v2/tabular_recovery/diagnostics/`.

7. **G-gaps, corrected.** G2 (footprint 350% diagonal), G5 (spread), G4
   (rebuild latency), G7 (level memory) are already in the matrix.
   Surviving builds, and only if 07 says they sit on the path: G1
   (delta-by-price histogram, still the one `reading-delta` p4–7 cannot be
   asked without), G10 (session CVD vs running median), G3 (digit-size
   distribution, partial), G11 (tick-run length after own-side aggression,
   the two-to-four upticks), older-balance registry beyond prior session.

## Principles that shaped this

- Encode lessons in structure: S6 is geometry joined to quote rebuild and
  memory reload, not `_seen` / `_age_sec` latch order.
- Subtract before add: do not build G1 until 08 misses the rung.
- Prove it works: the next receipts are
  `artifacts/entry_v2/tabular_recovery/diagnostics/{ceiling_split,scale_calibration,s6_occupancy}_20260822.json`.
- Exhaust the design space: Dawes vs sequence vs S6-then-rank. Dawes lost
  on measurement. S6-then-rank stays fog.
- Model the domain: a state machine, not a score bag. Illegal: a later
  stage without the earlier ones.
- Never block on the human: engineering forks (sequence vs Dawes, event
  clock vs Δ grid) are taken here.
- Sequence work into verifiable units: 07 ∥ 09 ∥ 10, then 08 only if 10
  shows S6-complete above the shuffle band.
