## The result that IS solid: the score was inverted

The frozen spec read the per-side extreme as "short: largest aligned, long:
smallest". Run 1 cashed HG long +$1,272 and short -$1,408 — a clean mirror, not
an edge in one direction.

`disc_auction_*_vwap_aligned_usd` is built as `side * (mid2 - 2*vwap)`
(`engine/entry_v2/discretionary_features.py:948`), and `probe_extension_prior.py`
derives the same convention independently: `ext = -aligned(own level)`, so
"beyond the level on the fade side" is aligned < 0 on BOTH sides.

All four side pairings are now cashed and the pairing is chosen on TRAIN only.
`long_min_short_min` won **24 of 24** asset x score-column x block cells. A knob
fitted to TRAIN does not win every held block of every asset on both score
columns, and the feature source says the same thing. This is a sign-convention
correction.

Under it, Stage A goes from $1,441 / $1,214 / $1,442 (missing every rung) to
$2,760 / $1,824 / $2,378. **The candidate set already contains the money.** The
generator was never the bottleneck and neither was the model family.

## What the live gap is

Stage B captures 58% (HG), 42% (NKD) and 81% (SI) of its own Stage A oracle on
TRAIN. The oracle's whole remaining advantage is two things:

1. It knows which side pays. Stage B enters the first side whose clock fires.
2. It never has to stop waiting.

So the live gap is a side-selection question and a stopping question. That is a
much smaller problem than "the information is not on this plane", which is what
tickets 25 and 26 had left standing.

## The blocker on every dollar figure here

`cash_is_age180_proxy`. Cash is the selected name's y at 180 s of age, but the
rule enters at `extreme_t + H` with H of 120 or 180 minutes, and this matrix
samples each series only to 300 s of age. On a fade of an extended move, the
drift you wait through IS the reversion you are paid for, so the sign of the
correction is unknown and its size is plausibly larger than SI's $59 FORWARD
margin. Ticket 29 prices it. **Until it lands, no number in this document may
be quoted as an economic result, and no downstream lever is worth running,
because every one of them would be tuned against a proxy.**
