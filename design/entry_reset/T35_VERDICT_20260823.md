## The frame, and why it is the right one

The score is static per name: its own 180 s row. So the name that ends a phase
as its side's extreme necessarily BEAT every earlier name when it became
eligible. Define a **new-extreme event**: a name that sets a new running extreme
on its own side at its own eligibility moment.

Two consequences, and they are the point:

1. **The event set contains the paying name with recall 1.000** — measured on all
   three assets, not argued. Every ticket-28 Stage A leg pick is an event.
2. **Every event is entered at 180 s of age**, which is the only age this matrix
   labels exactly. The unpriceable 2-hour wait (T29) disappears by construction.

Ticket 28's hold was identifying this same name two hours late and could not be
priced. Ticket 34 showed the arming TIME carries nothing on its own. This frame
keeps the identity and drops the wait.
