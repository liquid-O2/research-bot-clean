## The verdict

**The rule is real and it replicates. It is also about half the rung.**

Frozen on TRAIN before any held block was read, then read once:

Every asset clears its null on every block, which is the part that matters: a
causal, prefix-legal, exactly-labelled entry rule that pays about $800-1,060 per

Nothing in this program has replicated like that before.

Every asset also letters `loc_insufficient`. HG needs 2.3x, NKD 1.6x, SI 1.4x.

## What the arms taught

- **BEST_SINGLE beats every composite.** One column, `disc_prior_high_aligned_usd`
  (further beyond the prior session's high on the fade side), wins on NKD and SI.
  The 17-level and 3-level unit-weight composites (LOC_Z) cash $841 and $1,048
  against BEST_SINGLE's $875 and $1,465. The family is one signal measured many
  times, and averaging it dilutes rather than strengthens.
- **HG has no surviving level at all** (0 of 32 clear AUC 0.60 on TRAIN). Its
  best arm is MAX_BEYOND, the deepest extension across all 32 levels with no
  selection. HG's paying geometry is not prior-session location.
- **COUNT_CLEARED and NEAREST_BEYOND are near-worthless** ($-15 to $410). How
  many levels were cleared, and which one was cleared most recently, carry
  almost nothing. Depth does; count does not.
- **Abstention makes it worse.** Sweeping a quantile on the frozen score, cash
  falls monotonically on all three assets (SI $1,465 at q=0 to $524 at q=0.6),
  so q* = 0 everywhere. The cells the score calls weak are not the losing cells:
  the score's magnitude is not a quality signal, only its within-cell ORDER is.

## Two defects found and fixed in this ticket

- **The freeze rule was a gate-not-goal defect.** It froze the arm with the
  highest TRAIN cash without requiring that cash to beat its own TRAIN null,
  which put HG on LOC_Z_ALL at $1,740 against a $2,307 null and then spent a
  held read on it. Now an arm inside its TRAIN null is not a candidate at all
  (`arms_outside_null`, fixtured, mutant red). Under the corrected rule HG
  freezes on MAX_BEYOND, which is outside its null on all three blocks.
- **A dead branch in NEAREST_BEYOND**: the `isfinite` guard could never fire
  because the min is taken over a `where(..., inf)`. Removed rather than left as
  a branch a reader has to reason about.

## Where the goal stands

The chain, each link measured this session:

1. The side convention was inverted; correcting it moved the finished-cell
   oracle from missing every rung to clearing HG's by 2.1-3.2 SE.
2. The payer is always a new-extreme event (recall 1.000), and events are
   entered at 180 s, so their cash is exactly labelled.
3. The event oracle clears HG and SI at 2.7-3.3 SE: $2,772 / $1,851 / $2,396.
4. Extension beyond the prior session's high separates the payer among events,
   on TRAIN, THRESHOLD and FORWARD.
5. Live, that rule banks $857-1,061 on held blocks — 31-51% of the oracle
   against the 63-81% the rungs need.

**The gap is now a factor of 1.4 to 2.3 in capture, on a rule that replicates.**
It is not an identification gap (a real signal is found), not a pricing gap
(cash is exact), and not a generator gap (the oracle clears).
