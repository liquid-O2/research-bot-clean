# Ticket 52 — cell richness IS predictable, causally, at the cell's start

Log `artifacts/cache/t28_logs/regime.log`. 2021 TRAIN + THRESHOLD. The frame is
new: every previous scan asked which NAME wins inside a cell. This asks how big
the CELL will be, from the FIRST event's own row, before the cell has revealed
anything.

## Why this frame

The confirmed HG finding: the price-extreme score ranks usefully in cheap cells
(payer at percentile 0.309) and is a coin flip in rich ones (0.502, chance),
against a null band of +/-0.11. The money is in the rich cells. So the live
question is not "which name" but "is this cell going to be big", and it has to
be answerable at the cell's start or it is useless.

Ticket 19 measured a version of this and shelved it: realized phase range
correlates with cell-max at Spearman 0.82 BETWEEN cells. But realized range is
known only at phase end. This ticket asks the causal version.

## The answer: yes, and the survivors are one coherent family

Spearman across cells, first event's row against the cell's best y. Null floor
is the 97.5th percentile of |rho| under a shuffled target.

**NKD** — 63 TRAIN cells, 39 THRESHOLD, null floor 0.262, **28 survivors**:

| Column | TRAIN | THRESHOLD |
|---|---|---|
| `w1800_event_rate` / `w1800_event_count` | +0.523 | +0.468 |
| `disc_tclock_n512_sweep_speed_ticks_per_sec` | +0.493 | +0.362 |
| `disc_vclock_v1024_sweep_speed_ticks_per_sec` | +0.466 | +0.358 |
| `disc_prior_range_usd` | +0.465 | +0.346 |
| `disc_regime_h1800_path_variation_usd` | +0.426 | +0.388 |
| `disc_auction_session_value_width_usd` | +0.425 | +0.272 |
| `disc_fvol_session_range_consumption_usd_per_min` | +0.419 | +0.352 |
| `w1800_path_variation_usd` | +0.395 | +0.501 |

Every one of those is **activity, speed or width**: how busy the tape is, how
fast price sweeps, how wide yesterday ranged, how much path the last half hour
covered. Volatility begets extension, and it is legible at the cell's start.

**SI** — 32 TRAIN cells (null floor 0.383, the small-sample cost), 54 survivors,
led by `disc_tclock_n512_path_variation_ticks` +0.548 / +0.295 — the same family.

**HG** — only 3 survivors, and the family is nearly absent:
`disc_auction_session_directional_profile_skewness` +0.453 / +0.233 and
`disc_regime_h60_path_variation_usd` -0.301 / -0.217.

## The caveat that must travel with this

`phase_remaining_sec` (+0.423 HG, +0.653 SI) and `phase_index` (+0.591 SI) are
CLOCK columns. More time left means more room for a big move, so they will
correlate with cell size for a reason that is capacity, not regime. That is the
tautology that ate ticket 26 and they are flagged, not promoted. **The finding
rests on the activity family, which is not clock.**

## What is established, and what is not

**Established:** a causal, between-cell signal for cell richness exists on NKD
and SI, from columns already on this matrix, holding TRAIN and THRESHOLD in the
same direction at 0.35-0.52 against a 0.26-0.38 floor. Nothing in this program
has measured this frame before.

**Not established:** that it converts to dollars. A conditioner that says "this
cell will be rich" does not by itself say which name to take there — and rich
cells are exactly where the price-extreme picker is chance. The two-regime rule
needs both halves and only one is in hand.

**HG is the problem case.** It has the highest rung, the confirmed coin-flip in
rich cells, and almost no regime signal. Whatever works on NKD and SI may simply
not exist for copper on this plane.

## Next

Cash it. Split cells by the conditioner on TRAIN, and measure the picker's
payer-percentile and its dollars separately in each regime. If the picker is
informative in the predicted-cheap cells and chance in the predicted-rich ones,
the two-regime split is real and the open question narrows to "what ranks names
in a rich cell". If the split does not reproduce out of sample, the conditioner
is a correlation without a use.
