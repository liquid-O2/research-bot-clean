# Experiment and failure history

> **Historical scope notice.** This document describes the earlier IWM/Russell
> clean-room research and its exact scientific objects. It is not the current
> Entry V2 futures result or execution cursor. For the 2026-08-16 through
> 2026-08-18 Entry V2 attempts, including v3/v4 learned failures, v5/v8/v9
> engineering refusals, the six real-path rehearsal failures, and the fact that
> the replacement learner never ran, use
> [`ENTRY_V2_CURRENT_STATUS.md`](ENTRY_V2_CURRENT_STATUS.md).

This is the human-readable history. Exact task-card, code, input, output and
review hashes belong in `knowledge/evidence.tsv` and `evidence/claims/`.
Numbers below must not be read outside the disposition and scientific object
stated for their section.

## 1. Preserved production authorities

The earlier pipeline produced event publication, candidate/action books,
outcome panels, entry-path targets, opportunity sets, fold registry, cost
tables and oracle diagnostics. Those are preserved read-only as external
authorities. They are not automatically valid feature or selection rosters
for the new causal learner.

The economic kernel established the once-only 576-cent round-trip cost and
adverse-side executable fills. Later audits repeatedly reproduced the
certificate authority and caught consumer-side MAE derivation errors.

## 2. Aggregate and path-falsifier era

Several tests used whole-second tensors, curated aggregates, selected active
rosters, or side-marginal models. They were useful for plumbing and for
identifying that prior implementations lost chronology, side roles, native
option structure and conditional interactions.

Their null or weak results are `SUPERSEDED` for the broad learnability
question. They remain evidence only for their exact representations. They do
not prove a raw-data information ceiling.

Important defects discovered:

- timestamp/side or action-key joins were sometimes under-specified;
- current partial seconds could be consumed unless explicitly excluded;
- opening-second left padding was wider than exact-open rows alone;
- side-blind experts followed by latent sign flips did not perform correct
  own/opposite role orientation;
- some nullable context fields were zero-conflated;
- denominator rows could be dropped when targets/actions were unavailable;
- self-consistent local receipts were not sufficient protection against
  coordinated source/receipt substitution;
- hash/ID-based scientific subsampling was invalid;
- slow-volatility and cross-root inputs had scope/fold or channel-authority
  blockers and were typed absent/rejected.

## 3. Fresh entry label and stop decomposition

A shared dense cache was constructed for fresh entry opportunities with exact
decision keys, strict-after fills, certificate outcomes, fixed marks, barrier
states and stop-related fields. Pass A and Pass B were subjected to identity
checks and evaluator-key audits.

An independent stop audit found 77 consumer MAE mismatches caused by using an
incorrect fallback for no-positive-pre-stop paths. The sealed $300
certificate MAE was correct. Exact nonprimary $200/$400/$500 path MAE was not
available in the shared schema, so those breach panels were globally marked
`BLOCKED_NONPRIMARY_STOP_PATH_MAE` rather than guessed.

Disposition: the $300 economic authority is useful, but hindsight certificate
selection/exit remains `NONPROMOTABLE` causal evidence.

## 4. Causal raw-prefix model tournament controls

A small direct/temporal/interaction architecture was frozen with exact
chronological sampling, train-only normalization, keyed full denominators,
future-leak and XOR controls, source/leaf binding and fail-first mutations.
Static review found and repaired:

- reversed temporal normalizer column order;
- wrong head used for certificate and barrier AUC;
- per-row CUDA synchronization in temporal gather;
- stale smoke paths that did not execute the corrected production route;
- missing live tensor-receipt binding;
- source and prediction-key authority gaps.

The three-epoch future-outcome leakage control initially failed its
preregistered AUC threshold: F4 certificate AUC about 0.9609 and F5 about
0.9773, although top-score precision was 1.0 and losses were still falling.
This was diagnosed as undertraining of the same multi-task path, not absence
of the injected signal. A clean, preregistered ten-epoch rerun passed:

- F4 certificate-head AUC about 0.99812;
- F5 certificate-head AUC about 0.99923.

These are `POSITIVE_CONTROL` results only. They demonstrate that the
architecture, optimization and score path can learn an explicitly injected
future answer. They contain no causal alpha.

The episode also showed why controls must share the exact head, architecture,
optimizer budget, denominator and scoring path used by science.

## 5. Oracle and fixed-exit diagnostics

Certificate-oracle value was substantially higher than value from the same
entries held to a fixed 15-minute mark. A separately reselected fixed-15
hindsight oracle could also be high, showing that entry selection and exit
selection are coupled; one cannot swap exits and retain the oracle claim.

A corrected truth-coverage calculation showed that a frozen one-percent
economic gate was infeasible even under perfect certificate-net ordering:
approximately $576.56/session on F4 and $657.20/session on F5. Only the
ten-percent coverage diagnostic cleared $1,600 in both folds. This refuted
the gate, not the existence of causal signal.

Disposition: `NONPROMOTABLE` ceilings and evaluator diagnostics.

## 6. Delayed same-side hindsight opportunity

A dual-pass cached authority measured what happened if one waited fixed
delays before taking a same-side executable opportunity prior to the original
certificate exit. The claim was explicitly narrowed because the cache did
not prove detector-episode identity.

After independent physical rehash and reconstruction, headline diagnostic
means were:

| delay | F4 $/session | F5 $/session | F4/F5 retained opportunity | F4/F5 MAE breaches >$300 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 7,467.48 | 9,136.61 | 100% / 100% | 0 / 0 |
| 30s | 2,151.73 | 2,594.43 | 15.92% / 15.00% | 142 / 142 |
| 60s | 2,010.86 | 2,338.65 | 11.91% / 11.07% | 64 / 61 |
| 90s | 1,850.37 | 2,142.89 | 10.10% / 9.65% | 51 / 43 |
| 120s | 1,719.44 | 1,868.69 | 9.58% / 7.80% | 41 / 30 |

The fixed delays retained hindsight value but lost most opportunities and
violated the zero-breach risk law. The “optimistic ceiling” was a
per-opportunity chooser with different collision behavior and was not a
global schedule upper bound.

Disposition: `NONPROMOTABLE` hindsight opportunity authority. It motivated
ENTER/WAIT/PASS, but did not test whether causal raw state could learn it.

## 7. Native causal-learnability formulation

Repeated refute-first reviews progressively removed invalid shortcuts:

- final cluster/disposition/size and matrix-selected roster;
- same-side proxy for episode identity;
- decision-ordinal/decision-second clock conflation;
- persisted option side, sweep and flow fields;
- equal-time attachment acceptance;
- invalid stock trade condition classes;
- locked/crossed/one-sided quote signing;
- incomplete full-window order carrier;
- realized-MAE selection;
- trade-IID risk certification;
- under-specified equal-time group prior state and bin boundaries.

The resulting task card admits primitive candidates at their own visibility,
authenticates candidate/member physical-event identity, uses prefix-only
sets, preserves exact recent groups and a complete 120-second ordered
carrier, defines typed dependencies, freezes a representation/interaction
ladder, and uses continuous checkpoint-own value with auxiliary events and a
separate risk head.

Disposition at cleanup: reviewed formulation, no scientific fit.

## 8. Production reader adapter failures

Direct Python Parquet decoding, copied private helper formulas and legacy
`OptionPrintReader` were rejected. The legacy option reader both opened
forbidden persisted fields and omitted required causal fields. A dedicated
in-crate safe Rust reader was specified.

Successive no-payload adapter prototypes were rejected before market
execution for:

- forbidden/insufficient projections;
- failure to retain typed null physical rows;
- nonfinite/unrepresentable values aborting rather than typing invalid;
- UTF-8 timestamp slicing panic;
- input/output symlink and canonical-path escape;
- self-attesting rather than frozen source/build admission;
- non-replayable absolute-path patch provenance;
- insufficient production-constructor fixtures and field-state censuses;
- wrong-civil-day non-null quote attachments aborting the leaf instead of
  retaining the print with typed invalid dependency.

The last V2 closure closed the earlier seven P0s but remained `REFUTED` on the
wrong-civil-day attachment issue. No raw probe was launched. This is the
current Gate-1 implementation starting point, not active production code.

## 9. Process failures to avoid

- Spending substantial time on oracle/exit diagnostics after the user asked
  for causal feature learnability.
- Treating infrastructure closure as equivalent to progress on the economic
  goal.
- Using weak summaries or toy controls to adjudicate a native-state question.
- Serializing independent work and leaving GPU/CPU idle without a measured
  reason.
- Repeated one-defect-at-a-time card revisions instead of consolidated
  ambiguity audits.
- Reporting hashes and internal machinery without first stating the plain
  scientific outcome.
- Allowing stale, rejected or merely newest files to look authoritative.

The clean-room repository exists to make those failures mechanically harder.
