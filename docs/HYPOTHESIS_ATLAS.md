# Hypothesis atlas

Every item below is `UNTESTED` unless the evidence ledger says otherwise.
The purpose is to preserve the full research space without confusing ideas
with results.

## Microstructure and conditional-flow hypotheses

1. Candidate reversals are valuable when quote withdrawal precedes them and
   refill/stock prints confirm afterward.
2. Option directional pressure has value chiefly when stock tape and NBBO
   state agree; marginal option aggregates can look weak.
3. Fast option activity without underlying confirmation is more likely
   inventory/volatility demand than directional information.
4. Persistence and acceleration of own-side pressure matter more than total
   volume.
5. Conflicting option rights, expiry bands, or strike/moneyness regions may
   distinguish hedging from informed direction.
6. The same reversal magnitude has different value by candidate age, local
   range state, time of day, liquidity, and volatility/surface regime.
7. Waiting 30–60 seconds can improve state observability but sacrifices many
   opportunities; ENTER/WAIT/PASS must learn the trade-off rather than use a
   fixed delay.

## Volatility and IV hypotheses

- IV is primarily a state and interaction variable, not necessarily a
  directional marginal.
- Surface shape, dispersion and change may matter more than level.
- Quote-side IV and print-side flow must be aligned by lawful attachment
  clocks, expiry, strike and right.
- Missing/stale quote structure is a typed market state, not a zero IV.
- A scalar aggregate or sessions lacking native quote coverage cannot close
  IV-mediated mechanisms.

## Dynamical-systems hypotheses

- Hawkes/self-exciting intensity may represent burst/confirmation structure.
- Change points may identify transitions in liquidity or flow regimes.
- Rough-path signatures or CDEs may retain irregular path ordering and
  cross-modal areas lost by pooled summaries.
- Koopman/HAVOK latent state may expose transient dynamics.
- A committor-like head may estimate reaching a profitable executable state
  before the adverse stop.
- Time-reversal discrimination may measure path irreversibility.
- Non-normal operators may generate transient amplification despite stable
  marginal dynamics.
- Bicoherence may expose nonlinear phase coupling across modalities.
- Chronological analog retrieval may identify recurrent causal states if
  exact prior-only support and distance calibration are enforced.

## Required adjudication template

For each hypothesis, freeze:

- causal mechanism;
- raw fields and dependency clocks;
- smallest preserving carrier;
- baseline and added arm;
- positive control;
- matched destruction;
- metric and Holm family;
- cost budget;
- exact inference permitted by a positive or null result.

No family advances because it is mathematically sophisticated. It advances
only if it adds out-of-fold value under its measured budget and survives
leave-one-family-out.
