# Reasoning framework

## The unit of reasoning

Treat every experiment as a claim about one fully specified scientific
object:

| Dimension | Required declaration |
|---|---|
| Population | sessions, denominator rows, eligibility and typed exclusions |
| Clock | decision time, observation time, strict-prior/equal-time law |
| Modalities | exact raw sources, columns, dependency clocks and masks |
| Representation | ordering, grouping, windows, transforms and availability |
| Target | exact outcome, horizon, censoring and loss masks |
| Action | side, ENTER/WAIT/PASS semantics and tie law |
| Exit/risk | fixed or dynamic exit, $300 stop, gap and MAE |
| Execution | fill kernel, one-position occupancy and 576-cent cost |
| Validation | fold registry, embargo, calibration and contamination status |
| Selection | finite family, multiplicity correction and cost gate |
| Evaluator | uncapped replay and complete daily/concentration panels |

A result transfers to another object only through a separately justified
inference. Similar names do not establish equivalence.

## Goal-backward workflow

1. **Contract.** Freeze the deployable decision and joint economic pass law.
2. **Mechanism.** Write a falsifiable causal story, including why timing,
   persistence, phase, or cross-modal context should matter.
3. **Observability.** Map every mechanism variable to strictly prior raw
   fields and typed unavailable states.
4. **Carrier.** Choose the smallest representation that preserves the
   proposed mechanism.
5. **Destruction.** Remove only the information asserted to matter while
   preserving clocks, marginals, masks, and denominator.
6. **Positive control.** Prove the production architecture and loss can learn
   a known signal on the same path.
7. **Cost probe.** Measure real constructors, full feature path, model
   throughput, memory, output, and parallel scaling.
8. **Development evidence.** Run preregistered folds and Holm-corrected rank
   gates; keep/kill provisionally.
9. **Ablation.** Finalize family value by leave-one-family-out, not isolated
   marginal lift alone.
10. **Economic replay.** Evaluate the selected causal policy with exact stop,
    fills, occupancy, costs, zero days, and concentration.
11. **Confirmation.** Use the one-shot confirmation fold only after the
    family and thresholds are frozen.

## Refute-first questions

Before execution, try to prove:

- a roster row is selected by future or final state;
- a clock admits equal/current/future data;
- a dependency-derived field remains unmasked when its source is invalid;
- a side mapping changes under a same-side replacement episode;
- an “order” model can pass using availability or marginal leakage;
- a control uses a different head, path, optimization budget, or denominator;
- a threshold, horizon, exit, or target was chosen after outcome inspection;
- a report can pass after a leaf/source/receipt substitution;
- an output can be partially published or overwritten;
- an aggregate result is being described as a raw-data conclusion.

Execution begins only after deliberate mutants demonstrate each refusal.

## Evidence vocabulary

- **ACTIVE:** current lawful evidence for its exact object.
- **SUPERSEDED:** valid historical evidence replaced for an explicitly stated
  reason.
- **REFUTED:** a claim or implementation contradicted by a test or audit.
- **CONTAMINATED:** valid only as exploratory/burned evidence.
- **NONPROMOTABLE:** diagnostic or hindsight evidence that cannot support
  deployment.
- **UNTESTED:** specified hypothesis without a lawful result.
- **BLOCKED:** decision-complete but unable to execute because a named
  authority or implementation condition is absent.

“PASS” without one of these dispositions, the full object tuple, and exact
hashes is not a scientific conclusion.

## Feature-family value gate

For each family, preregister:

- mechanism and expected sign/conditionality;
- exact carrier and maximum columns;
- build and inference op counts;
- projected and measured wall/RSS/VRAM/output;
- destruction and positive controls;
- primary rank metric and Holm family;
- maximum 20% pipeline-wall share;
- provisional keep/kill and final leave-one-out adjudication.

Do not exhaustively grid transforms or coordinates. Choose a small
mechanism-complete set, then kill families that do not repay their budget.

## Confidence and the 90% request

The requested “over 90% guarantee” is interpreted as an empirical readiness
probability, not certainty. It requires:

1. a frozen finite policy family;
2. complete out-of-fold daily ledgers under the deployment evaluator;
3. block resampling at the session/regime level, not IID trades;
4. the joint event mean > $2,000 and MDD < $1,000, with literal zero selected
   MAE breaches above $300 and all concentration conditions;
5. a conservative lower confidence bound above 0.90;
6. independent confirmation without reopening selection.

No teacher AUC, oracle value, single-fold result, or subjective confidence may
be reported as this probability.
