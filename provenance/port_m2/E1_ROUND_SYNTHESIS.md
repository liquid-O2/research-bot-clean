## 3. THE INSTRUMENTS AND THEIR LAWS

**THE MIRROR LAW (CC-M2-13.1, minted by this reader on day 4).** A direction term must beat its own
MIRROR on **every** session, not merely be positive on every session. It explains all of the round's
direction failures at once and it has never been passed by any object in this library.

**THE POOLED-POOL LAW (day 3, ERA_NOTES §33/§41).** Settle a threshold on the pooled candidate pool,
never on the replay or on the set of days it makes positive.

**THE SEAT-SPENDER LAW (CC-M2-17.4, generalised day 8).** Grade every refusal AND every concentrator
on the sub-population that would have spent a seat, not on the pool. Four consecutive $0.00 replay
deltas and one inverted concentrator are its receipts.

**THE ANCHOR LAW (CC-M2-19.1, day 7's break).** A state read at a cell's open is 4-8 hours stale on
an 8.5-hour phase. Any feasibility or regime state must be ROLLING — evaluated at the row being
called.

**THE COMPOSITION ORDER (CC-M2-19.2, measured three sessions running).** SIDE > SEAT > MOMENT.
Oracle side is worth +$2,030 to +$5,494 a session; oracle seat alone -$268 to +$244; the moment is
not free (40% capture on a right-cell-right-side).
